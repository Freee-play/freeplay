// Why You Shouldn't Skip Class
A few reminders before you head out
01

Missing Key Explanations

Teachers often drop crucial hints about upcoming tests or break down complex problems in ways that textbooks just can't match.

02

Snowballing Catch-Up Work

Skipping one single class turns into spending hours later trying to track down notes and teach yourself from scratch.

03

Attendance & Participation Points

A lot of classes bake attendance directly into your grade. Skipping means throwing away free percentage points.

04

Breaking the Routine

Once you break the habit of showing up, it gets way easier to justify skipping the next time, leading straight into a slump.
